Artifact Resonator

Welcome to the Alien Artifact Analysis Lab.

Strange relics keep arriving from deep space. Each one pulses with unstable energy — and inside that energy is a maths puzzle.

The unknown energy level is written as the alien symbol Ω (think of it as the usual “x” in algebra).

Your job: decode the equation, find the exact value of Ω, and stabilize the artifact before it overloads.

What kind of puzzle is this?

You will see equations with fractions, like this:

Sample transmission
  1. Fractions make the equation messy.
  2. The trick students learn: multiply every term by a smart number so the denominators cancel.
  3. Then solve the simpler equation for Ω.

That “smart number” is usually the LCM (lowest common multiple) of the denominators — for 2 and 4, that is 4.

How to play — three steps

  • 1. Choose a Frequency Multiplier — tap a number (try the LCM of the denominators), or type a custom one.
  • 2. Fire Multiplier — the beam multiplies both sides of the equation. You only have a few charges (the glowing battery pips).
  • 3. Calibrate Ω — type the answer (integer or fraction like −21/5), then press Stabilize Artifact.

Correct answer: the artifact locks and you earn Energy Crystals. Wrong answer: you still get a full worked solution so you can learn, then scan the next relic.
