Remainder Radar: Submarine Sonar Hunt. A Primary 4 and 5 game about finding hidden numbers from remainder clues. Solo uses the mouse, keys 1 to 4, the arrow keys, and Space. Co-op shares one team score and three hull shields. Player 1 uses keys 1 to 4. Player 2 uses Q W E R. Player 3 uses U I O P. In Sonar Duel each lane has its own riddle, score, and three lives. Press M to mute. Press Escape to pause.
