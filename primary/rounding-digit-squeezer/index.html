Digit Squeezer

A factory that teaches rounding. Each round picks nearest ten, hundred, or thousand at random.

Step 1. Read the banner. Whack that place. Nearest hundred means the hundreds digit.
Step 2. Whack the digit immediately on its right. Nearest thousand means the hundreds digit.
Step 3. If that digit is 0–4, pull Roll Down. If it is 5–9, pull Boost Up. A 9 rolls over — 79 999 becomes 80 000.
