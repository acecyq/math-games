Lilypad Leap

Frog Sync Race

Frogs hop the pond in different sized jumps. Find the first lilypad they all land on together. That number is the Lowest Common Multiple.

Example: hops of 4 and 6 meet at 12, 24, 36… The lowest is 12.

4 rounds each pond · 3 frogs from pond 3 · up to 48

P1 · RedChoose: left / right
Lock: spacebar
8BitDo 1: D-pad + A
Or click a lily
P2 · BlueChoose: A / D
Lock: F
8BitDo 2: D-pad + A
P3 · YellowChoose: J / L
Lock: H
8BitDo 3: D-pad + A

A bigger shared pad is a trap — a turtle reminds you. A pad only one frog can use makes a splash and costs a heart. First correct lock scores +150.
