AM/PM Oven Timer

Run the sunrise shift. Jump the hours first, then the minutes, and flip AM to PM when the timeline crosses noon or midnight. 12:00 pm is noon. 12:00 am is midnight.

Timeline jump method

Ending timeAdd the hours first, then the minutes. 9:10 am + 2 h 20 min lands on 11:10 am, then 11:30 am.
Starting timeSubtract the hours first, then bridge back through :00. 4:30 pm − 1 h 50 min is 3:30 pm, then 2:40 pm.
DurationBridge to the next hour, count the hours, add the leftover minutes. 10:45 am to 1:15 pm is 2 h 30 min.
