Barnyard Balance: Two-Pen Picture Equations. A Primary 5 and 6 game about forming two picture equations from cute farm animals and taking one animal off the board. Solo uses the mouse, keys 1 to 4, A and D with Space, or gamepad 1. Co-op shares one team score and one life pool. Player 1 uses 1 to 4, A and D, and Space. Player 2 uses Q W E R, J and L, and Enter. Player 3 uses U I O P, the arrow keys, and Shift. In a Barn Race each farmer has a separate tally, score, and three hearts. Press M to mute. Press Escape to pause.
