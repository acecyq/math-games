Shuriken Intervals

Solo is one ninja. Co-op and Duel need 2 or 3. In co-op the score is the team’s only — nobody keeps a private tally.

Dojo constructs

Gaps = targets − 1Distance from the 1st board to the Nth is the gaps times one spacing.
Targets = gaps + 1Boards at both ends of a wall: divide, then add the last board.
Cuts = pieces − 1Equal slices of bamboo need one fewer slash than the number of pieces.
Loop, climb, reboundA closed courtyard has gaps = boards. The last climb does not slip. A bounce travels up and down.
