Squishy Bakery

How many bakers are playing?

• Each player gets their own station, orders, and score.
• Shared 90-second timer.
• Read carefully: portion of stock, mass in kg (1 kg = 1000 g), or how much more when the recipe needs more than you have.
• 8BitDo: each player claims a pad, then D-pad + A to answer.
• Keyboard (Player 1): keys 1–4.

Claim Your Controller

Each player: press A on your own 8BitDo (X-input mode).

Player 1 can also play with keyboard only.
