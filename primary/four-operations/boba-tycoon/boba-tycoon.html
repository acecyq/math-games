Boba Tycoon: Multi-Step Tea Shop. Singapore Primary 4 and 5. Bulk cartons to cups, bundle buying and selling, starting cash, and markup prices. Solo uses the mouse, keys 1 to 4, arrow keys with Space, or gamepad A. Co-op shares one team score and five shop stars. Player 1 uses 1 2 3 4. Player 2 uses Q W E R. Player 3 uses U I O P. In a Boba Shop Duel each shop has its own score and stars. A wrong calculation drops one star. Press M to mute. Press Escape to pause.
