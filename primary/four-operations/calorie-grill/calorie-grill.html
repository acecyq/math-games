Calorie Grill: Burger Bar-Model Kitchen. A Singapore Primary 3 and 4 game about comparison bar models, more and fewer calories, times as many, equal trays, and combo meals. Solo uses the mouse, keys 1 to 4, arrow keys with Space, or gamepad 1. Co-op shares one team score and one heart pool. Player 1 uses 1 2 3 4. Player 2 uses Q W E R. Player 3 uses U I O P. In a Burger Duel each chef has separate stars and lives. Press M to mute. Press Escape to pause.
