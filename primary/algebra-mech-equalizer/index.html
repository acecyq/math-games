Mech Equalizer: The Unknown Core. Teach algebra by assembling a mech, then balancing an equation. Player 1 uses arrow keys and space, or controller 1 button A. Player 2 uses W A S D and F, or controller 2 button A. Player 3 uses I J K L and H, or controller 3 button A. Move a reticle over an answer and confirm. Wrong answers cost one shield and pause that engineer for 2 seconds. Press M to mute the music. Set an 8BitDo pad to XInput so the bottom A button is button 0.
