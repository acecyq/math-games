Build each product the way you would on paper: one digit at a time, carries in the cups, zero seals so each new row keeps its place, then add the columns.

Tier 1: 9 × 5, 23 × 8, 478 × 6. Tier 2: 29 × 35, 169 × 83, 7,639 × 42. Tier 3: 478 × 391, 8,395 × 601, 7,492 × 5,738.

  • Pick the rune for the whole step, after adding any carry orb.
  • A shifted row needs zero seals first: tens 1, hundreds 2, thousands 3.
  • A wrong rune cracks the parchment and costs that artificer 1 shield. Each artificer has 3.
  • The fastest correct stamp earns a knighthood star. A true product launches the trebuchet for +250 honor.
