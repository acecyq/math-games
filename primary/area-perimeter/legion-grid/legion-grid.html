Legion Grid Phalanx Area & Shield Wall

Scout the enemy formation. Area is every soldier standing on the grid. Perimeter is one shield on every open edge. A corner soldier guards two edges. A courtyard needs a second wall.

How a wave works

  1. Step 1 — count the soldiers (area). Rows × columns, minus any gap, or add the joined battalions.
  2. Step 2 — count the shields (perimeter). One shield on every open edge. Corners guard two edges. A hollow courtyard adds an inner wall.
  3. A corner notch removes soldiers and keeps the outer rectangle’s shield count. A gap in the middle of a side adds 2 × depth shields.

Solo. Mouse, keys 1 2 3 4, or Left / Right and Enter. Gamepad 0: D-pad and A.

Co-op. One team score and shared hearts only. P1 takes soldiers, P2 takes shields, then the lead rotates through P1, P2, and P3. P1 keys 1–4 or pad 0. P2 keys Q W E R or pad 1. P3 keys U I O P or pad 2.

Duel. Everyone races the same formation. The first correct lock takes the star and the speed bonus. A wrong lock is a 1.5-second stun. Mouse belongs to P1.

Scale. 1 soldier = 1 cm. Each exposed edge is 1 shield.
