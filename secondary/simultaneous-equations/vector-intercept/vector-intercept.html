LINEAR BEAM GRID

Two linear beams cross on a Cartesian radar. Plot each equation with two lattice points, then lock the exact intersection — or recognise parallel beams and coincident beams.

STEP 1 · CYAN

Place two points that satisfy Beam 1. The cyan laser fires across the grid.

STEP 2 · MAGENTA

Place two points that satisfy Beam 2. Check a point by substituting into the equation.

STEP 3 · LOCK

Lock the crossing (x, y). Parallel beams have no intersection. The same line has infinite solutions.

SOLO

Mouse snaps to the grid. WASD or arrows move. Space or Enter plots and locks. Keys 1–4 fire cards. 8BitDo pad 1: D-pad or stick, A locks, LB/RB cycles cards, X fires the card.

CO-OP

P1 red reticle: WASD + F/Space, pad 1. P2 blue reticle: arrows + Enter, pad 2. Operators swap beams each round. The score is the team score.

VERSUS

Same controls. P1 cards are keys 1–4. P2 cards are 7, 8, 9, 0. A lock counts only after that player has plotted both beams. 8BitDo works best in XInput / standard mode.

Rounds 1–2 stay in the first quadrant. Rounds 3–4 cross into negative coordinates. Rounds 5–6 use fraction forms and half-unit intersections. Round 7 onward mixes parallel beams, coincident beams, and parameter recovery. A wrong point costs one shield. Every third intercept in a row restores one shield. Press 1, 2, or 3 to launch.
