Equation Annihilator: Term Crusher. A collider game that teaches the elimination method for simultaneous linear equations. Solo uses the mouse, WASD or arrow keys, and Space or Enter, or gamepad 1. In co-op and versus, player 1 uses WASD and F or Space, or gamepad 1. Player 2 uses the arrow keys and Enter, or gamepad 2. Press M to mute. Press Escape to pause.
