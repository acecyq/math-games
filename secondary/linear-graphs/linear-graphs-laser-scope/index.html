LINEAR INTERCEPTOR

Drone sensors sit on a straight line. Calibrate the y-intercept, tune the gradient, then fire along y = mx + c.

1 · BASELINE

Read y when x = 0. That vertical intercept is c. The turret clamps to (0, c).

2 · GRADIENT

m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). Watch the sign. Positive climbs. Negative drains.

3 · ESTIMATE

Substitute into y = mx + c, or rearrange to find x. The correct beacon vaporizes the wave.
