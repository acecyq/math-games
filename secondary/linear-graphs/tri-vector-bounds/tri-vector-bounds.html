TRI-VECTOR BOUNDS

COORDINATE AREA ARENA

Laser lines close a containment field. Find each vertex from the equations, then the exact area. The arena is a sketch only — no numbered grid — so coordinates come from algebra, not from reading the picture.

1 · APEX

Set L₁ equal to L₂ (or substitute a vertical line). Lock vertex B.

2 · BASE

Substitute the floor y = k or the wall x = h into each slanted line. Lock A and C.

3 · AREA

Triangle: ½ × base × height. Trapezium: ½ × (top + bottom) × height. Parallelogram: base × height.

Controls — Solo: mouse click, WASD or arrows, Space or Enter, gamepad 0 D-pad and A. Co-op and Versus — P1: WASD + F or Space, or gamepad 0. P2: arrows + Enter, or gamepad 1. Shields refill every field. Three misses in a field open the worked solution. 8BitDo uses standard X-input (D-pad + A).
