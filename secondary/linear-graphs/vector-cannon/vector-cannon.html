VECTOR CANNON

LINEAR GRAPH DEFENSE

Read enemy drones as ordered pairs (x, y), park the railgun on the y-intercept (0, c), and dial the gradient m = rise ÷ run. One laser is the whole line y = mx + c. On the hard tiers that single line has to pierce two drones at once.

DIFFICULTY

MATCH TIMER

MODE

1 · (x, y)

Horizontal x first, then vertical y. Mind the signs in all four quadrants.

2 · INTERCEPT c

Slide the turret on the y-axis. c is the height at x = 0, never the x-intercept.

3 · GRADIENT m

m = rise ÷ run. Uphill is positive. Downhill, left to right, is negative.

4 · TWO POINTS

m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁), then c so the line passes through either drone.
