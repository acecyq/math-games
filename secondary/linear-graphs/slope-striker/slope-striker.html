SLOPE STRIKER

GRADIENT & INTERCEPT LAB

Calibrate a Cartesian railgun grid. Start from a plotted point, measure rise over run to find the gradient, then lock the line equation y = mx + c. Later rounds add intercepts, unknown parameter k, collinear points, and the fourth vertex of a parallelogram.

01 · PLOT

Snap the reticle to a point on the grid: a beacon, the y-intercept, or vertex A.

02 · GRADIENT

Measure rise ÷ run from that point. That ratio is the gradient of the line you are building.

03 · LOCK

Fire the full law: y = mx + c, an axis intercept, the value of k, or vertex D. +350 navigator points.

SOLO

Mouse snaps to every 0.2. WASD or arrows step the reticle. Space, Enter, or Fire plots. Keys 1–4 choose cards. 8BitDo pad 1: D-pad or stick, A fires, LB/RB cycles cards.

CO-OP

P1 is the red reticle: WASD, F or Space, pad 1. P2 is the blue reticle: arrows, Enter, pad 2. Roles swap each round. The HUD shows only the team score.

VERSUS

Same controls. P1 cards are 1–4. P2 cards are 7, 8, 9, 0. A wrong lock costs that player one shield. M mutes. Esc pauses. 8BitDo works best in XInput.

No gamepad yet — keyboard and mouse are ready. Every round is generated fresh. The last 6 problems cannot repeat.
