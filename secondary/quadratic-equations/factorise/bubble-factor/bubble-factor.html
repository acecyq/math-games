Algebraic Identity Pop

Singapore Secondary 2 · Recognise special identities, then factorise them. Pop the bubbles you earn.

Step 1 · Identify Perfect square sum (a + b)², perfect square difference (a − b)², difference of squares (a + b)(a − b), or not a special identity.
Step 2 · Factorise If it is an identity, lock the exact factors. A correct “not an identity” call skips straight to the volley.
Step 3 · Pop Four bubbles load into the launcher. Bounce off the walls, snap to the hex grid, and pop 3+ of one colour. Floating clusters avalanche.
Lookalike traps x² + 25 is a sum of squares. A wrong middle term (4x² + 6x + 9) is not (2x + 3)². A negative constant is not +b². Extract a common factor before the identity.
