How the rally works

1 · Read the slotThe flashing blueprint slot is the next term. Square, cross-term, then square — in that order.
2 · Line up and blastSteer the rocket so your shot meets the correct panel. Keys 1–4 glide you toward that row.
3 · Punch throughThe right panel scores +150 and opens the wall. A wrong blast or a crash costs 1 life. Finish the identity for +400, and the next wall comes in faster.

Identities in play

Square of a sum(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
Square of a difference(a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
Difference of squares(a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²
General binomial(px + q)(rx + s)
