How a cake is boxed

1. Read the edgesThe glowing side term multiplies the glowing top term. That product is the area of the slice.
2. Frost every sliceA 1×2 bar, a 2×2 sheet, or a six-slice banquet. Negative × negative is positive. x × x is x².
3. Collect like termsAdd matching slices, then write the total in descending powers. A leading minus flips every term.
