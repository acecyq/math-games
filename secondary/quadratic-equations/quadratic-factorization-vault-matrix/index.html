Vault Matrix: Quadratic Grid Lock. A three-player arcade game that teaches factorisation of ax squared plus bx plus c with the 2 by 2 grid method. Player 1 uses arrow keys and space, or gamepad 1. Player 2 uses W A S D and F, or gamepad 2. Player 3 uses I J K L and H, or gamepad 3. Press M to mute. Press Escape to pause.
